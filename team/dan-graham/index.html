Dan Graham

Co-organizer

Dan is a Co-organizer of SIRA.

Dan works in technology risk and AI governance at State Street, focusing on responsible AI and the governance of generative and agentic AI systems. His work covers generative AI risk and controls assessment for applications, supporting systems impacted by Project Glasswing. Dan’s research interests span topics including the normalization of deviance in AI development, prevention and detection of generative AI CSAM, and compute governance.

Dan holds an MS in Computer Science and a BS in Cognitive and Brain Sciences from Tufts University, where his academic work included investigating inter-group bias in facial recognition within reinforcement learning systems. He previously served as an outreach coordinator and executive board member of the Tufts AI Safety Student Association (TASSA) as well as an executive board member and client manager for JumboCode, a student software development organization providing pro bono services to Massachusetts nonprofits.
