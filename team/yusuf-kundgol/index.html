Yusuf Kundgol

Co-organizer

Yusuf is a Co-organizer of SIRA.

Yusuf is an Operations Associate at ARENA, a program of the London Initiative for Safe AI, where the Operations team helps the program achieve its maximum impact. Yusuf studied Philosophy, Politics and Economics at the University of Oxford.

Yusuf writes about AI safety, including the normalisation of deviance in AI development, model consciousness, and what counts as recursive improvement in intelligence.

SIRA

SIRA studies the societal impacts of advanced AI systems to understand whether society is prepared for the transition to AGI and the world that follows.
