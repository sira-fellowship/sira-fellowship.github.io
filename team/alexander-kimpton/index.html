Alexander Kimpton

Co-organizer

Alexander is a Co-organizer of SIRA.

Alexander is a geopolitical risk and strategy analyst at Dragoman, specialising in China and technology policy, and has worked with clients across defence, energy, technology, pharmaceuticals, and life sciences. Alexander’s research focuses on the political economy of AI development: what is both implementable and preferable given the incentives facing states and AI labs. Current work examines the normalisation of deviance in how labs and states manage risk, and what preserves human autonomy and bargaining power as AI advances.

Alexander holds master’s degrees in political science from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, with a thesis on China’s national AI strategy, and in international relations, and is a PIBBSS fellow in Cape Town. Alexander is based in Melbourne.

SIRA

SIRA studies the societal impacts of advanced AI systems to understand whether society is prepared for the transition to AGI and the world that follows.
