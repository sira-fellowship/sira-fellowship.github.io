Roshni Lulla

Co-organizer

Roshni is a Co-organizer of SIRA.

She is a cognitive neuroscientist who studies how emotion and moral values shape decisions, and applies that work to AI safety. Her research bridges affective neuroscience, moral cognition, and AI misalignment. She is Co-Founder and Head of Research at the Institute for Humane Robotics, where she works on guidelines for safe human-robot interaction. She holds a PhD in Brain and Cognitive Sciences from the University of Southern California, where she was advised by Antonio Damasio and Jonas Kaplan at the Brain and Creativity Institute. She brings more than ten years of research experience in consumer behavior, along with experience as an entrepreneur, and has spoken at Wharton, Affectiva, and AI safety conferences.

SIRA

SIRA studies the societal impacts of advanced AI systems to understand whether society is prepared for the transition to AGI and the world that follows.
