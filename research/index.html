Research

Our research studies how advanced AI is changing the foundations of society, before and after AGI.

Research agenda

We take a broad view of what counts as a societal impact. Each area below can be studied from any discipline, empirically or theoretically.

Work and income

How AI changes the work people do, the value of their skills, and how the economic gains are shared.

Minds, beliefs, and decisions

How AI shapes the way people think, learn, form beliefs, and make choices, individually and together.

Relationships, wellbeing, and meaning

How AI affects people’s relationships, mental health, and sense of purpose, and the social ties that hold communities together.

Institutions and governance

How the institutions societies depend on, from democratic bodies to systems of accountability, adapt to AI or fail to.

Power and wealth

How AI affects who holds economic and political power, and what keeps that power in check.

Geopolitics and international order

How AI shifts the balance of power between states, and how countries compete, cooperate, and govern AI across borders.

How we work

Understand

Study effects already underway, such as shifts in work and growing reliance on AI for judgment, using empirical and theoretical methods.

Anticipate

Think ahead to the transition to AGI and the world after it, and assess which social structures are most at risk.

Inform

Publish research and risk assessments so the public, governments, and companies can make informed decisions.

Publications

Our first publications are in progress. In the meantime, the evidence page collects more than 250 published studies on AI’s societal impacts. Researchers interested in this agenda can express interest in our research team or the research fellowship.

SIRA

SIRA studies the societal impacts of advanced AI systems to understand whether society is prepared for the transition to AGI and the world that follows.
