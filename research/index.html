Research

Our research measures how advanced AI is changing the foundations of society, before and after AGI, and tests what would help society adapt.

Research agenda

A first outline of the questions we focus on. We will refine it with researchers in the field and publish an updated agenda in 2027.

Work and income

How AI changes which tasks people do, what their skills are worth, and how income and bargaining power are shared. Which jobs and tasks is AI already changing, and who bears the cost? If AI can do most cognitive work, what would cushion the transition?

Beliefs and decisions

How AI shapes what people believe and how they decide. How much do people defer to AI for judgment, and when does that go wrong? How do AI-generated content and persuasion affect the shared information that societies rely on?

Institutions and governance

How labor markets, democratic institutions, and systems of accountability adapt to AI, or fail to. Which institutions are most likely to bend or break under rapid adoption? How do organizations, including AI companies and states, come to normalize risk over time?

Power and wealth

How advanced AI could concentrate economic and political power, and what keeps it in check. Who gains and who loses bargaining power as AI grows more capable? Which checks on power are most likely to hold, during the transition to AGI and after it?

How we work

Measure

Track effects already underway, such as shifts in labor markets and growing reliance on AI for judgment, with rigorous empirical methods.

Project

Project those effects forward to the transition to AGI and beyond, and assess which social structures are at risk.

Test

Test which interventions help people and institutions adapt, so that decisions can rest on evidence.

Publications

Our first publications are in progress. In the meantime, the evidence page collects more than 250 published studies on AI’s societal impacts. Researchers interested in this agenda can express interest in our research team or the research fellowship.

SIRA

SIRA studies the societal impacts of advanced AI systems to understand whether society is prepared for the transition to AGI and the world that follows.
