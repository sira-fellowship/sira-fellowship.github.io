Why donate?

A missing piece

Most AI safety work measures what AI systems can do. Far less studies what happens as they are deployed widely and become part of how people live, how economies work, and how institutions make decisions. SIRA is building an independent research organization to measure these effects, before and after AGI.

Why now

The transition may be a period when choices harden quickly: who holds power, how institutions decide, and what people come to believe could become very difficult to reverse. The time to study these effects is while they are still taking shape, so that society keeps its ability to steer itself.

What your gift funds

SIRA is early, so gifts go a long way. Right now they fund our first pilot event, a weekend workshop hosted by Collider in New York on 23–24 January 2027; our first report on the state of the evidence; a research pilot with mentored projects across our focus areas; and the groundwork for our in-house research team and Research Fellowship.

Progress so far

311 researchers have expressed interest in our fellowship in the three weeks since launch, including at least 44 PhD students and 16 postdocs, and 8 mentors have committed. Our census maps more than 100,000 papers on AI’s societal effects (see the evidence).

How to give

SIRA accepts donations through Manifund, a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit. Manifund receives donations and makes grants to SIRA, so donations may be tax-deductible in the US.
