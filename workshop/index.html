Weekend Workshop

A two-day workshop for established faculty who want to study the societal impacts of advanced AI.

Boston or San Francisco • Dates to be announced

Express interest

AI is already changing how people work, learn, decide, and govern, and the transition to AGI could change them far more. Whether society is prepared isn’t decided yet, and very few researchers are studying what these changes mean for people and institutions, before and after AGI.

We’re looking for faculty with a track record of excellence in their field. Maybe you’ve run a long-running panel study, built a widely used survey instrument, modeled how markets or institutions change, or spent years inside the organizations you study. Whatever it is, our goal is to show you how your research can help society understand and prepare for advanced AI. The evidence page shows what that work already looks like.

Program

Workshop

Two days · Boston or San Francisco

Through talks, one-on-ones, and small-group discussions, hear from researchers studying AI’s societal impacts, exchange ideas with faculty from other disciplines, and build relationships across the field.

Optional extension

A few months · Remote

Stay connected with your cohort and researchers in the field, scope a project on AI’s societal impacts in your area, and find ways to bring in your students, including through SIRA’s fellowship.

Explore your fit

You’re a great fit if you:

  • Hold a faculty position (assistant, associate, or full professor) in any discipline
  • Lead an established research program; many participants will come from the social sciences, such as psychology, economics, sociology, political science, and cognitive science
  • Take seriously that advanced AI could transform society, for better or worse
  • Have engaged with some writing on AI’s societal impacts or AI safety, or want a structured place to start
  • Keep an open mind and are willing to admit when you’ve been wrong
  • Want your research to shape how AI is developed and used

The workshop is built for mid-career researchers. Undergraduates, graduate students, and postdocs should look at the fellowship. Faculty who want to spend a full program on a project can apply to the fellowship’s senior track.

FAQ

How much background in AI do I need?

You should understand why advanced AI could transform society and why that matters. Specialized technical knowledge is not required.

What will the weekend include?

Invited talks, small-group discussions, and one-on-one conversations with researchers studying AI’s societal impacts, including academics who have already moved into the field.

How much will it cost?

We aim to make the workshop free to attend, with travel, accommodation, and meals covered for the in-person weekend.

When and where is the workshop?

We plan to host it at an AI safety hub in Boston or San Francisco, close to universities with strong AI safety communities. Dates will be announced once the workshop is confirmed.

Who are the speakers?

Speakers will include researchers and professionals working on AI’s societal impacts and AI safety, as well as academics from many disciplines who have already moved into the field.

Know someone who would be a strong fit?

Email us at contact@sira-project.org with their name and why you think they’d be a good fit.
