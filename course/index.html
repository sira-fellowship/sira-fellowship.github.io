Course

A self-guided introduction to the social science of AI safety.

Express interest

There is no single place yet for a social scientist to learn how their field connects to AI safety. This course aims to be one: a self-paced set of readings, open questions, and research methods that you can work through on your own schedule, with pointers to where to go next, including the SIRA fellowship and workshop.
