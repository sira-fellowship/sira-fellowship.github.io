SIRA Style Guide

For slides, documents and anything else that carries the SIRA name. One typeface, one ink colour, four accent colours taken from the logo.

Logo

Full logo

Title slides, cover pages, letterheads. Spells out "Societal Impacts Research on AI".

Horizontal logo

The everyday version: content slides (small, bottom corner), headers, email.

On dark backgrounds

Use the -dark files (lighter colours, cream text) on night or other dark grounds.

The mark

The four rows spell S-I-R-A in Morse code, one colour per letter. The telegraph was one of the first technologies to reshape society in real time.

Do keep clear space around the logo of at least one dot's height. Use the -soft files on light backgrounds and -dark on dark ones.

Don't stretch, rotate, recolour, outline or reorder the mark, or rebuild it from shapes.

Instrument Sans

The only typeface. Free from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com/specimen/Instrument+Sans).

Google Slides: Font menu → More fonts → search "Instrument Sans".

PowerPoint / Keynote: download and install it first. If that isn't possible, use Helvetica or Arial.

Rules of thumb

  • 16:9, white background by default.
  • Ink for text; accents for emphasis, charts and small details, not big blocks of colour.
  • Sentence case for titles ("How AI is changing work", not "How AI Is Changing Work").
  • Write "SIRA"; spell it out once on the title slide only.
