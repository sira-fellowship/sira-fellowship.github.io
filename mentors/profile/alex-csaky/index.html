Anthropology

About

Alex is an AI safety researcher working on AI for epistemics and decision-making: how AI can help people form better beliefs, make wiser decisions, and coordinate. Alex co-authored a report on the state of the field of AI for epistemics and coordination, and is now looking at how to put these tools to use in the real world. Alex studied archaeology and anthropology at the University of Oxford and is interested in how anthropology’s research methods and goals can be applied to AI safety and to navigating toward good futures.

Research directions

  • AI for epistemics and decision-making
  • Putting AI tools for epistemics and coordination to use in the real world
  • Applying anthropological research methods and goals to AI safety

Recent work
