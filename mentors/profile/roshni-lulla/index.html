About

Roshni is a cognitive neuroscientist who studies how emotion and moral values shape decisions, and applies that work to AI safety. Her research bridges affective neuroscience, moral cognition, and AI misalignment. She is Co-Founder and Head of Research at the Institute for Humane Robotics, where she works on guidelines for safe human-robot interaction, and a co-organizer of SIRA. She holds a PhD in Brain and Cognitive Sciences from the University of Southern California, where she was advised by Antonio Damasio and Jonas Kaplan at the Brain and Creativity Institute.
